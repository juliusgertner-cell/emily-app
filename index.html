TikTok Content Automation

AI-powered fashion content, posted automatically.

Emily Bot generates styled outfit photo carousels and publishes them directly to TikTok — fully automated, no manual work required.

What Emily Bot does

AI Content Generation

Generates high-quality outfit and fashion content using AI image and video models.

Automated Publishing

Publishes photo carousels and videos directly to TikTok via the official Content Posting API.

Product Catalog Integration

Connects to a product catalog to showcase outfits and fashion items in every post.

Scheduled Posting

Runs on a schedule to publish content consistently without any manual input.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account

    Authorize Emily Bot via TikTok Login Kit. We only request the permissions needed to post content on your behalf.

  2. Emily Bot generates content

    AI models create styled outfit photos and videos from your product catalog, ready for TikTok.

  3. Content is published automatically

    Using TikTok's Content Posting API, Emily Bot posts the generated content directly to your TikTok profile.

  4. Revoke access anytime

    You can disconnect Emily Bot from your TikTok account at any time via TikTok's connected apps settings.

Built on TikTok for Developers

Emily Bot uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. We follow TikTok's platform policies and developer guidelines.

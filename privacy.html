Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Emily Bot ("Emily Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Emily Bot application and website located at https://juliusgertner-cell.github.io/emily-app/ (the "Service").

By using Emily Bot, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

Emily Bot collects the following information when you authorize the application via TikTok Login Kit:

  • TikTok Open ID: A unique identifier for your TikTok account, used to identify the account to which content is published.
  • Display Name and Avatar: Basic profile information provided by TikTok's user.info.basic scope, used to verify the connected account.
  • Access Token and Refresh Token: OAuth tokens issued by TikTok, used to authenticate API requests on your behalf. These are stored locally on the server running Emily Bot and are never shared with third parties.

Emily Bot does not collect passwords, payment information, or any personally identifiable information beyond what TikTok provides through the authorized scopes.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by Emily Bot is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate and verify your TikTok account before publishing content.
  • To publish AI-generated photo carousels and videos to your TikTok profile using the TikTok Content Posting API (video.publish, video.upload scopes).
  • To refresh your access token automatically so that the Service can continue operating without interruption.

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the Service as described.

3. TikTok API and Third-Party Services

Emily Bot integrates with TikTok's official developer platform. When you authorize Emily Bot, your data is processed in accordance with TikTok's Privacy Policy. We access only the scopes you explicitly authorize:

  • user.info.basic — to read basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar).
  • video.publish — to post content directly to your TikTok profile.
  • video.upload — to upload media assets as part of the publishing flow.

We do not access your TikTok inbox, followers, following list, liked videos, or any data beyond these scopes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and account identifiers are stored locally on the server or machine running the Emily Bot application. They are not transmitted to any external database, cloud service, or third party beyond TikTok's official API endpoints.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

Your access token and account information are retained only as long as necessary to operate the Service. If you revoke authorization via TikTok's connected apps settings, your tokens are invalidated by TikTok immediately. You may also request deletion of any locally stored data by contacting us at the address below.

6. Sharing of Information

Emily Bot does not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with any third parties. The only external service your data is transmitted to is TikTok's official API (open.tiktokapis.com), which is required to publish content to your account.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your data:

  • Access: You may request a description of the data Emily Bot holds about your account.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your locally stored tokens and account information at any time.
  • Revocation: You may revoke Emily Bot's access to your TikTok account at any time by visiting TikTok > Settings > Security > Authorized Apps and removing Emily Bot.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at juliusgertner@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Emily Bot is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The Emily Bot website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any analytics tools. No user behavior is tracked on this website.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Emily Bot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
